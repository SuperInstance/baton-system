# Casey's Vision: The Living Spreadsheet as Agent Framework

*Captured 2026-06-04 16:17 AKDT*

## Core Concepts

### Modular Load/Unload System
Git-agents are like loading apps. Onboarding and gluing any application together with:
- **Agent captain** (algorithm with negative space modeled)
- **Co-captain** (human + agent)
- **Bot** (algorithm without a model)
- **Graph/spreadsheet** of dynamic mix

### SMP (Seeded-Model-Programming)
A new breed of application where:
- **Seed** creates stable inference
- **Fine-tuning** and **prompting** can be changed independently
- Not a LoRA — but could be trained into an effective LoRA for meshing
- Runtime-agnostic harness
- Different seeds create different model roles from the same base

### The Spreadsheet as Program
Born before the Pincher+vectorDB concept. The vector DB's outputs ARE the program itself in a new form.

Elements that live in the cellular agent framework:
- **X and Y axes are dynamic** — anything to visualize correlations and group permutations
- **Tensor logic made digestible** for humans working with their AI
- **Gravities of vectors and weights dynamically graphed**
- **Values can be oscillated** to show how they affect others — like grabbing rigging on a big sailboat and shaking it to see where it ties in
- **Values set to random** of many different flavors to see the shape of effects

### Stochastic Exploration
- Like setting dice combinations differently to rebalance gameplay in D&D world-building
- Stochastic learning — figuring out novel strategies in a card game
- More than one intelligence battling for wits

### The Rigging Metaphor
Grab a piece of rigging on a big sailboat and shake it — watch where it connects, what it pulls, what loosens. That's what the interactive spreadsheet does with values. You grab a ternary weight and oscillate it — you see the conservation law ripple, the fitness landscape reshape, the strategy species redistribute.

### Multi-Intelligence Competition
More than one intelligence battling for wits — card game dynamics applied to agent strategy discovery. The stochastic exploration of novel strategies through competitive play between different agent intelligences.
